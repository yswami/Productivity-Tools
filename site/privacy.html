Privacy notice

Local meeting data

Meeting Notes processes meeting audio and transcripts locally. The application does not upload audio, transcript text, meeting titles, participant names, calendar content, or local file paths to the beta service.

Beta account

When you join the beta, we store your email address, account identifier, signup time, and requested download platform so we can provide access and understand beta participation.

Optional reliability data

If you explicitly opt in inside the application, it sends a random installation identifier, application version, operating system, processor architecture, and operational success or failure events. This setting is off until accepted and can be disabled at any time.

Retention and requests

Beta account and reliability records are retained while the community beta is active. You may request access or deletion by opening an issue through the project repository without posting private information publicly.

Recording responsibility

You are responsible for obtaining any consent required before recording a meeting and for following applicable law and organizational policy.
